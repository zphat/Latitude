LATITUDE 0.3.0 — WINDOWS x64 BETA

Extract the entire ZIP. Open Latitude.exe. Keep the surrounding files with it.
No Node.js, OBS, account, or separate encoder installation is needed.

1. Connect and test your microphone, or skip. Camera is always optional.
2. Connect Screen A. Click a source in the preview to move it; drag a corner to resize.
3. Scene layers has exact position, size, Crop this source, fit, mirror, visibility and lock.
4. Add source adds text, gradients, images/logos, color panels and video layers.
   Ctrl Z undoes; Ctrl D duplicates; arrows nudge; Shift nudges 10 pixels.
5. Templates are optional. Review desk compares two crops of A; View B can instead
   capture another window or monitor. Each scene/layer has its own crop.
6. Voice Studio: run Check, pick a preset, and try Rich clarity around 25–35%.
   If raw input clips, reduce the microphone/interface's hardware gain.
7. Settings has five themes, 720p through 4K, 30/60 fps, quality and preview controls.
   Start at 1080p/30 and check a short take before recording an important episode.
8. Stop to save. Takes > Export creates an MP4 editing pack and synchronized WAV stems.

CapCut: put MP4 and WAVs at the same timeline start; mute the mix when using stems.
Optional per-track MP4 copies let each clip use mic-only or desktop-only audio.
Preview editing locks during recording; Unlock preview permits deliberate live edits.
The fps indicator measures compositing, not guaranteed encoded performance.

README.md covers routing, recovery, and limits. BUILD-REPORT.md lists checks.
The app is unsigned. Windows hardware capture and long sessions still need real testing.
