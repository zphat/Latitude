# Latitude 0.9.5 — name your takes, finish with less waiting

- Name your session beside **Start recording** or in the top bar. You can rename the current recording there too. Blank names get a readable local date and time instead of another “Untitled session”.
- In **Takes**, click a title or the explicit **Rename** button. The Export dialog also lets you change the name without losing your format, track or loudness choices. Existing recording folders, media and editor project links stay in place.
- **After recording** offers automatic editing files or **Save originals · export later**. Originals are saved first in either mode. Canceling an export keeps them available in Takes.
- Video exports share the normalized mix between the movie and editing WAV, and write processed stems in the same encoder pass. Raw backups use independent copy-on-write copies where supported, with a normal-copy fallback.
- Final loudness verification uses a lighter EBU R128 scan of the actual exported mix, including true peaks. Normalization, live voice processing, video quality, isolated tracks and cancel/recovery protections remain intact. No new runtime dependency or background processing loop.

Export duration still depends on your processor, drive, recording length and chosen format. H.264 capture can usually copy the picture into MP4; converting video formats and making per-source video copies take longer. See the [measured comparison and QA limits](https://github.com/zphat/Latitude/blob/main/docs/qa/session-names-0.9.5.md).

Windows remains an unsigned beta; Linux is a Flatpak preview. Apple Silicon and Intel Mac downloads are **unsigned, unnotarized DEVELOPMENT PREVIEWS**, require **macOS 14.2+**, and use **manual updates**. Native runner tests and packaging do not certify physical screen/mic/camera/system-audio capture, permissions or long sessions. A signed, hardware-tested Mac beta remains a separate milestone.

Windows/Linux: **Settings → App updates**, or install the matching release download. Mac: save, quit and replace the app in Applications using the matching DMG. Recordings, projects and preferences are retained. Mac encoder sources/notices and **SHA256SUMS-macOS.txt** accompany its downloads; **SHA256SUMS.txt** covers Windows/Linux.
