# Latitude 0.9.8 · Creative library

The optional Editor now has an offline **Library** beside its project actions.
Browse 12 transitions, 12 color looks, 12 editable title styles, and 16 font choices
(15 bundled open fonts and system monospace). Search, favorite, apply a style or
add a title at the playhead. Everything remains adjustable in the Inspector.

- New vertical slides, reverse/vertical wipes, center, circle and diamond reveals.
  Linear or smooth motion timing. Overlap pictures for a transition between them.
- More color looks, sepia and hue rotation. Native export now uses matching sRGB
  filter math instead of differently defined YUV brightness/contrast/saturation.
- **My packs** saves, imports and exports small data-only preset collections.
  Applied settings stay in the project, even after removing the pack. Local packs
  are marked unreviewed; they cannot declare themselves approved or execute code.
- Rename clips independently of their media files; search imported media. Open
  Inspector sections stay open when changing values. Undo/Redo keeps a surviving
  clip selected and clears an inspector pointing to a removed layer.
- Font loading is local and on demand. Closing the library releases its card
  canvases; only one hovered transition animates, and reduced motion is respected.
  Disabling the Editor removes its decoded fonts, listeners and media previews.
  Static post-entrance slide segments skip unnecessary per-pixel alpha work.
- Fonts and upstream notices add about 2.3 MiB. No new runtime dependency,
  framework, webview, transcription model or duplicate media decoder.

The included packs are free. Paid listings, purchases, downloadable font/animation
assets and clip-art packs are **not live**. The future catalog/trust/compatibility
plan is documented in [Creative packs](../CREATIVE-PACKS.md). Editing, manual
controls and exports remain available without a purchase or account.

Original recordings, voice DSP and recording/export formats are unchanged.
Color-adjusted exports may look different from 0.9.7 because they now match the
editor preview. This is a simple sRGB workflow, not a color-managed HDR grading
system or a full keyframe/animation editor. Save a copy of important projects
before trying a new beta; older Latitude versions do not understand the new styles.

Windows remains an unsigned beta; Linux an x86-64 Flatpak preview. macOS is an
unsigned, unnotarized DEVELOPMENT PREVIEW requiring macOS 14.2+, with MANUAL updates.
Native automated editor/export/package checks are separate from physical-device
capture, permissions, long takes, GPU behavior and update-installation testing.
