Latitude is a local screen recorder built around podcast audio and separate, synchronized tracks for editing.

## Download and start

Download **Latitude-0.3.0-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the entire archive, and open **Latitude.exe**. Keep the surrounding files with it. The packaged app includes its runtime and encoder; no Node.js, OBS, account, or separate FFmpeg installation is required.

The app is unsigned. Test a short recording on your hardware before using it for an important session.

## Highlights

- Drag, resize, crop, reorder, and lock sources directly in the scene editor.
- Add titles with gradient colors, local images, and color panels; undo composition edits.
- Connect one or two microphones, desktop audio, and an optional facecam.
- Use voice presets, EQ, compression, de-essing, expansion, limiting, and optional Rich clarity.
- Export the full mix and isolated voice/desktop WAVs, plus optional video copies with individual soundtracks for CapCut.
- Pause, add markers, switch scenes with hotkeys, and recover interrupted takes.
- Choose five themes and recording formats through 4K/60; actual performance depends on your hardware.

## Verification and limitations

21 automated tests passed, alongside synthetic recording/export and scene-editor checks. Short browser tests achieved approximately 30/60 fps at 1080p. The CPU-only test host did not sustain 4K/60. Real Windows capture, drivers, external hotkeys, and long-session stability still need hardware testing.

One webcam is supported. Multi-webcam capture, VST/AU hosting, per-app audio capture, a finished streaming mod, and explicit hardware-encoder selection are not included. Limiters cannot repair audio clipped at the microphone/interface.

See README.md for setup and CapCut workflows, BUILD-REPORT.md for evidence, and MODDING.md for extension boundaries.
