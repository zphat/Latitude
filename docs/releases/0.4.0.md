Latitude 0.4 is a Windows desktop beta focused on easier installation, reliable separate audio tracks, and lower processing overhead.

## Install and open normally

Download **Latitude-0.4.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe** and double-click it. Open Latitude afterward from your **desktop or Start menu**. No terminal, source folder, Node.js or separate FFmpeg is needed. The installer runs for your account without administrator privileges. Close older copies before installing/updating.

Recordings remain in your selected folder (initially Videos\Latitude). Uninstalling preserves recordings and preferences. The optional ZIP remains available for portable use; keep its extracted runtime files together.

## What changed

- Unused audio lanes and disabled Rich clarity skip processing; idle audio contexts suspend. Static/hidden previews stop scheduling, neutral EQ bands skip work, meter calculations are reduced, and settings writes are debounced.
- Gain/mute changes preserve sync-delay buffers. Processing bypass crossfades; headphone monitoring has peak protection; device setup and recording start/stop have stronger race and timeout handling.
- Video frames are requested immediately after completed scene draws. The final CPU-only 1080p/30 test recorded about **30.1 fps**.
- Exports can be canceled without touching originals or earlier exports. Progress advances during long stages, metadata writes atomically, and recording/export do not compete for resources.
- Held clipping warnings with Reset peaks, Save and close, focused shortcut fallback, smaller windows, and reviewable technical details for feedback.
- Installer/shortcut/launch/uninstall checks in Windows CI, expanded tests, a beta feedback guide and a Linux roadmap.

## Verification and limits

**34 automated tests passed**, plus real browser recording/export, crop/editor, idle-resource and interface checks with no renderer errors. Audio stems stayed aligned; the synthetic exported mix measured −16.01 LUFS against the −16 target. A worklet-only benchmark took about 25% less time with one natural voice than 0.3. This is not a whole-app resource or OBS comparison.

The Windows publishing gate verifies install, shortcuts, packaged launch and uninstall before making these assets available. Actual Windows capture devices, routing, drivers and long recordings still need hardware feedback. Start with a short test; this beta is unsigned. **4K/60 is selectable but was not sustained on the CPU-only test host.** One webcam is supported. VST/AI restoration, multi-webcam recording, per-app audio isolation and a streaming mod are not included. Limiters cannot repair clipping at the microphone/interface.
