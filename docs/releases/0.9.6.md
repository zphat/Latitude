# Latitude 0.9.6 — reliable scrubbing and smaller files

- Drag the timeline ruler or playhead to preview while holding the mouse. **Scrub · C** lets you drag over clips without moving them; **Select · V** restores clip moving and trimming. A click selects the position you clicked instead of jumping to the clip's start.
- Preview waits for decoded pictures and coalesces rapid seeks, so old frames do not masquerade as the requested cut point. Raw takes get a temporary seek index without re-encoding. Fractional source cuts and negative audio encoder timestamps are handled consistently by preview and export.
- Editable frame timecode, one/ten-frame stepping, Home/End and **Fit** help find exact positions. Select/Scrub/Zoom/Fit remain visible. A compact editor header and minimum usable preview/track heights improve smaller windows; side panels and divider remain adjustable.
- **Picture quality / size** beside Record adds a Smallest option for podcasts/documents and shows estimated video storage. Existing quality preferences are retained.
- Export offers **Original**, **Balanced compression** and **Smaller file**, compressed-audio bitrates from 128–320 kbps, and optional lossless FLAC editing stems. **Use space-saving export settings** skips duplicate editing files/video copies and preserves your separate-source selection. Editor export also offers **Smaller file**.
- New processed audio defaults to 24-bit PCM (25% fewer bytes than float32 processed audio). Raw mic safety copies remain float32 and enabled by default. Settings can retain float32 processed audio or explicitly turn raw backups off. Existing recordings are unchanged.

Compression can take longer and can lose detail; actual savings depend on the footage. It does not delete originals or older exports. Raw-take indexing temporarily needs space for another compressed picture file. Heavy 4K/VFR footage may still need **Make lighter / compatible preview**. The editor remains an optional, locally running mod, with no new framework, runtime dependency or background decoder when disabled.
